    Calibrate

    1. Remove all load

    Hang the load cell the way you'll use it, with slings and carabiners attached but nothing pulling on it. Then tare.

    2. Hang a known weight

    Use something you know the exact mass of, ideally 10 kg or more (weight plates, a water jug you weighed). Let it stop swinging.

    Backup

    Download everything (settings, calibration, routines, workouts, holds, grips and all sessions) as one file, or restore from one. In the app the file opens Android's share menu: save it to Files or Drive, or send it. Your data is only on this phone, so keep a recent backup.
