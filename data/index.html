          Rep threshold

          During a routine without a target load, a rep counts as loaded while force is above this.

          Hangs with the sensor

          With the sensor, each hang waits until the force goes over the start level, then the countdown starts. Off: hangs start on the clock.

          The routine or test ends 0.5 s after you let go (force below the rep threshold), at most 5 s after the last hang, so the graph shows the release.

          Backup

          Download everything (settings, calibration, routines, workouts, holds, grips and all sessions) as one file, or restore from one. In the app the file opens Android's share menu: save it to Files or Drive, or send it. Your data is only on this phone, so keep a recent backup.
